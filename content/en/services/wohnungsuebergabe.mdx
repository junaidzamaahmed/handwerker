faq:
  - q: Do I really have to paint when I move out?
    a: >-
      Not automatically. In Switzerland normal wear is covered by the rent, and walls
      have a typical life of around eight years depending on the contract. Beyond
      that — lots of plug holes, coloured walls, smoke marks, damage — is on you. We
      tell you on the visit what we would do in your place. Your tenancy agreement
      and the handover protocol decide.
  - q: How short notice can you come?
    a: >-
      Half a day can usually be found within a week. In busy moving months — March,
      June and September — two to three weeks' notice is better.
  - q: Do you also do the final clean?
    a: >-
      No. Specialist firms with a handover guarantee do that. We do the trade work
      beforehand and clear our own mess.
  - q: Can you invoice the management?
    a: >-
      If the management commissions the work, yes. If you commission us, we invoice
      you — then you can show the invoice at inspection.

The most expensive way to hand a flat back is the one where the management
commissions the work itself and sends you the bill. The second most expensive is
doing everything on the protocol — including what is not on you. The difference is
half an hour walking through before the date.
