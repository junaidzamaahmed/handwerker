faq:
  - q: Sand or replace — which is better?
    a: >-
      As long as the wear layer is thick enough, sanding is almost always the better
      choice. It is cheaper, it makes no waste, and old oak usually looks better after
      sanding than a new covering. We tell you on the visit whether it still works.
  - q: How long can I not use the room?
    a: >-
      With floating laminate or parquet you can go back in the same evening. After a
      seal you should go easy on the floor for two days and wait about a week before
      laying rugs.
  - q: Will you lay material I bought myself?

      Yes. Tell us beforehand what you bought — with some cheap coverings we can only
      stand behind the result to a limited extent, and we say that before, not after.

      put them back.

An old strip floor from the 1930s often has more wear layer than a new engineered
board will ever have. Before we write a quote for a new floor, we tell you whether
sanding is still worth it — even if that is the smaller job.
