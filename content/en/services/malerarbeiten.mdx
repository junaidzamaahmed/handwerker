The difference between a good paint job and a bad one does not show on handover day.
It shows in raking light six months later: at the plug holes that telegraph through,
at the ceiling line, at the patch above the door frame. All of that is made before
the painting. That is why we price the preparation openly, instead of burying it in
the square-metre rate.
