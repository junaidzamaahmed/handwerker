faq:
  - q: How much ceiling height do I lose?
    a: >-
      With a shallow frame about 5 to 8 centimetres. Spots or thicker insulation take
      it to 10 to 15. We tell you beforehand what it will be in your case.
  - q: Can I hang something heavy on a plasterboard wall?
    a: >-
      If we know where in advance. For a television, wall cupboards or a basin we
      build a noggin or a timber plate into the studs. Afterwards it is special
      fixings and much less load.
  - q: How messy is it?
    a: >-
      Sanding makes fine dust. We work with an extracted sander and dust-tight
      screens. Not zero, but a large difference from working open.
  - q: Do you also do fire or wet-room boards?
    a: >-
      Yes. In bathrooms and kitchens we use impregnated boards; on ceilings to
      neighbours or attics, fire boards if you want them.

Drywall is the job you are not supposed to see. A good ceiling nobody notices; a
bad one shows every board joint as soon as the sun comes in low. The difference is
in the framing and the number of filling passes — both items missing from a cheap
quote.
