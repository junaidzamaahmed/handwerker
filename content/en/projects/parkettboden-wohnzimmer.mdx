On a floor laid in one direction, that direction decides how the room reads. Here
the boards run towards the windows, so the light travels with the grain and the
joints barely show.

The subfloor was checked and levelled before laying. That is the line missing from
cheap quotes, and the reason a new floor creaks after two years.
