With an open joist ceiling the easy solution is to board everything in — and the
nicer one is to leave the beams. The second is more work: every beam needs a clean
junction, and every joint between timber and plaster moves with the seasons. In
return the room still reads as an old building, not an office.

The electrical cable was pulled in before the boards. Anything else would have
meant opening the finished ceiling later.
