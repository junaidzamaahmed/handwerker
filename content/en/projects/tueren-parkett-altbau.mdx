The floor in this flat was about eighty years old and had more substance than any
engineered board you could have bought for it. Replacing it would have cost more
and taken the character out of the corridor.

In flats like this the visible difference usually comes from the woodwork. Freshly
varnished frames and skirting change a room more than a new wall colour — and they
are the work most often left out.
