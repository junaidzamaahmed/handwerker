## 2. Data we process

When you visit the website: IP address, date and time, page requested and browser
type in server log files, deleted automatically after 14 days.

When you send an enquiry through the form: name, phone number, email address,
postcode, your description and any photos.

## 3. Purposes and legal bases

We process this data to answer your enquiry, carry out the job and meet statutory
retention duties. The legal basis is performance of a contract or, where we ask
for it, your consent.

## 4. Form: nothing stored on this website

Your enquiry is not stored in a database on this website. It is delivered as an
email to our office and kept there as ordinary business correspondence.

## 5. Cookies and consent

We use only technically necessary storage to run the website and to remember your
cookie choice. Anything else we set only after your explicit consent. You can
change or withdraw that decision at any time.

## 7. Disclosure abroad

Where data goes to a country outside Switzerland and the EEA, we rely on the
standard contractual clauses or on an adequacy decision of the Federal Council.

## 8. Retention

Server log files: 14 days. Enquiries without a job: 12 months. Job-related
correspondence and invoices: 10 years under the Code of Obligations.

## 9. Your rights

You have the right of access, rectification, erasure, restriction of processing,
data portability and the right to object. Contact us informally at
{{privacy.controller.email}} or {{contact.phoneDisplay}}. You may also complain to
the Federal Data Protection and Information Commissioner (FDPIC).

## 10. Security

Transmission is encrypted over HTTPS. Access to enquiries is limited to the people
who handle the job.
